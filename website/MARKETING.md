# AsterMax Marketing System

## Brand position

**AsterMax is an auditable engineering-computation platform that combines deterministic FEA with a long-term AI engineering-agent architecture.**

AsterMax is deliberately not marketed as “AI that replaces the engineer”. The product promise is stronger and more defensible: make modelling intent explicit, keep the analysis tree editable, use numerical evidence as the authority, and add intelligence only on top of verified physics.

## Primary tagline

**Engineering Beyond Limits**

## Product-line tagline

**FEA verificable hoy. Agente de ingeniería mañana.**

## Governing message

> **AI proposes → the model is explicit → the engineer decides → the solver executes → benchmarks verify → AI interprets.**

## Three brand rules

- **No hidden physics.**
- **No invented evidence.**
- **No autonomy without validation.**

## Elevator pitch

AsterMax is building a new kind of CAE environment: a finite-element platform where the engineer can work manually through a fully editable analysis tree or, in the future, describe a mechanical problem in natural language and receive a transparent proposed model. Instead of sending language directly to a solver, AsterMax converts intent into an explicit engineering specification, exposes assumptions, executes deterministic numerical methods, verifies the result and only then uses AI to interpret the evidence.

## Core audiences

1. Mechanical and structural engineers who need transparent FEA workflows.
2. CAE users who want automation without losing manual control.
3. Industrial engineering teams performing RCA, design verification and load-case studies.
4. Researchers and open-source contributors interested in agentic engineering software.

## Product pillars

### Physics first

The numerical core is deterministic and benchmark-driven. Compilation never equals certification.

### Explicit engineering model

The analysis tree and Model Specification remain visible, editable and authoritative.

### Engineering AI

The long-term agent is intended to reason about modelling intent, boundary-condition hypotheses, applicable standards, time available and confidence required.

### Evidence first

Numerical validation, regression, equilibrium and adversarial review outrank agent confidence.

### Future multiphysics

Structural mechanics is the starting domain. Advanced thermal analysis, dynamics, CFD and FSI belong to the long-term roadmap and must not be described as current certified capabilities unless independently validated.

## Message architecture

### Problem

Traditional CAE software gives the engineer powerful numerical tools but leaves much of the modelling judgement implicit. AI tools can automate interaction, but automation without transparency can make modelling errors harder to detect.

### AsterMax response

AsterMax treats boundary conditions as engineering hypotheses, the tree as the source of truth and numerical evidence as the authority. The future AI agent guides model construction while preserving manual control.

### Differentiator

The goal is not “chat with a solver”. The goal is an **Engineering Intent Compiler**:

```text
Natural language
→ physical interpretation
→ explicit Model Specification
→ validation
→ visible analysis tree
→ engineer decision
→ deterministic solver
→ numerical evidence
→ AI interpretation
```

## Marketing accuracy policy

Every public artifact must distinguish among:

- **Available / numerically validated now**
- **In development**
- **Product vision / roadmap**

Future AI guidance, standards interpretation, adaptive-fidelity planning and multiphysics must never be presented as currently certified capabilities until their validation evidence exists.
