# Trademarks and naming

The GNU GPL governs copyright permissions in covered software; it does not grant a right to use third-party trademarks or imply sponsorship.

## AsterMax identity

AsterMax should maintain an independent name, logo, iconography, color system, documentation style, and user-interface identity. No claim is made here that the AsterMax name or logo is registered in any jurisdiction. Trademark clearance should be performed before a major commercial launch or registration effort.

## Third-party marks

ANSYS and Ansys Mechanical are marks associated with ANSYS, Inc. SOLIDWORKS and related Dassault Systèmes marks are owned by Dassault Systèmes or its affiliates. Other names belong to their respective owners.

Third-party names may be used in factual text when reasonably necessary to discuss compatibility, migration, benchmarks, comparisons, or historical workflow experience. Do not:

- put a third-party mark in the AsterMax product name, repository name, logo, domain, or package name;
- use a third-party logo as an AsterMax icon or badge;
- imply certification, sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation that does not exist;
- deliberately imitate distinctive third-party trade dress;
- market AsterMax primarily as a "clone" of a named proprietary product.

Preferred wording describes capability directly, for example: "parametric mechanical CAD workflow" or "integrated structural FEA workflow". Comparative references should be secondary and factual.
